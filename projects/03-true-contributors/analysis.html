The True Contributors: Who Actually Moves the Table?

Goals win points, but not all goals win the same number of points, and a goal is rarely one player's work. This study prices every goal by how many points it was worth to the team that scored it, shares that value with the scorer, the player who set up the shot and the player before him, and asks who adds the most per 90 minutes once luck and short careers are taken out.
